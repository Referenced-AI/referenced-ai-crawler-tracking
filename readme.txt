Counts visits from known AI crawlers and reports daily totals to Referenced.

== Description ==

Referenced – AI Crawler Tracking counts visits from known AI crawlers such as GPTBot and PerplexityBot on your site and posts hourly day totals to your Referenced account, so you can see which AI engines are reading your content.
